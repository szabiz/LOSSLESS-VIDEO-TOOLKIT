Container and audio/video format support (own code, no third-party code)
----------------------------------------------------------------------
The joiner / extractor code (MPEG-TS/PS, MP4/M4A, Matroska/WebM, and the raw audio formats AAC-ADTS,
MP3/MP2, AC-3/E-AC-3, Ogg Opus) is the program's own original code, written from publicly available
technical specifications (ISO/IEC 13818-1/-2/-7, 11172-3, 14496-3, 14496-12, RFC 8794, RFC 9559, RFC 3533,
RFC 7845, RFC 6716, ATSC A/52). No source code from FFmpeg, libavformat, libopus, libmpg123, LAME, FAAD or any
other project was copied or translated. The program does not encode or decode audio or video; it copies
the stream packets / frames unchanged. Patent and licensing questions of the codecs themselves (e.g. H.264,
MPEG-2, AAC, MP3, AC-3, Opus) concern the makers and users of codec implementations; seek legal advice if you
distribute this program as part of a commercial product.
